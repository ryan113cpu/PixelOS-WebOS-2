# PixelOS

Hey! Welcome to PixelOS.

PixelOS is basically a mini operating system that runs in your browser. You can open apps, move windows around, change the theme, save notes, make files, and do a few other things.

## How to use it

Open the PixelOS HTML file in a browser.

When it loads, you'll see the desktop with the app icons.

Just click an icon to open an app. You can also use the taskbar at the bottom.

To move a window, click and drag the top of it. To close one, click the red button in the top corner.

## The apps

###  Notes

The Notes app is just for writing stuff down.

Type something in the box and press **Save**. Your note gets saved in the browser.

**Clear** removes the saved note.

###  Calculator

The Calculator is for basic math.

You can click the buttons, and you can also use your keyboard to enter calculations.

###  Theme Lab

Theme Lab lets you change the way PixelOS looks.

You can change the accent colour, pick a different wallpaper, randomize the theme, or reset it back to normal.

###  Files

Files is a simple fake file manager.

Type in a file name and press **Create File**. The file will show up in the list, and you can delete it whenever you want.

These aren't real files on your computer. They're just files saved inside PixelOS.

###  Music

The Music app lets you play the music included with PixelOS.

Pick a track and press **Play**. Press **Pause** when you're done.

###  Settings

Settings has some of the main options for PixelOS.

You can change wallpapers, turn notifications on or off, change the sound setting, open Theme Lab, and reset the settings.

## Saving stuff

PixelOS uses your browser's local storage to save things.

So your notes, files, and some settings can still be there when you close and reopen PixelOS.

If you clear your browser's site data, the saved PixelOS information may be deleted.

## Notifications

PixelOS sometimes shows a little notification when you do something, like opening an app or saving something.

If you don't want them popping up, you can turn notifications off in Settings.

## Windows

You can have multiple apps open and move their windows around.

If a window is in the way, just drag it somewhere else.

## That's pretty much it

There isn't really a complicated way to use PixelOS. Just click around and try the different apps.

Have fun :)
