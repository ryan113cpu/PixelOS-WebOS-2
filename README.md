## PixelOS
Hey! Welcome to PixelOS.
PixelOS is a small operating system thing that runs in your browser. You can open apps, move windows around, change the theme, save notes, make files, and a few other things.
## How to use it
Open the PixelOS HTML file in your browser.
Once it loads, you'll see the desktop and the app icons. Click an icon to open an app, or you can use the taskbar at the bottom.
You can move windows by clicking and dragging the top of them. To close a window, click the red button in the corner.
## Apps
## Notes
Notes is pretty simple. Just type something in the box and press **Save**.
Your note gets saved in the browser, so you can come back to it later. **Clear** removes the saved note.
## Calculator
The Calculator is for basic math.
You can click the buttons to type something in, or use your keyboard.
## Theme Lab
Theme Lab is where you can change how PixelOS looks.
You can change the accent colour, choose a different wallpaper, randomize the theme, or reset everything back to normal.
## Files
Files is basically a fake file manager.
Type a file name and click **Create File**. It'll show up in the list, and you can delete it whenever you want.
The files aren't actually being created on your computer. They're just saved inside PixelOS.
## Music
The Music app lets you play the music that comes with PixelOS.
Choose a track and press **Play**. When you're finished, press **Pause**.
## Settings
Settings has some of the main PixelOS options.
You can change the wallpaper, turn notifications on or off, change the sound setting, open Theme Lab, and reset the settings.
## Saving stuff
PixelOS uses your browser's local storage to save things.
This means your notes, files, and some settings can stay there even after you close and reopen PixelOS.
If you clear your browser's site data, though, the saved PixelOS stuff might be deleted.
## Notifications
PixelOS sometimes shows a small notification when you do something, like opening an app or saving something.
If you don't want them, you can turn notifications off in Settings.
## Windows
You can have more than one app open at the same time.
If a window is in the way, just click and drag it somewhere else.
## That's pretty much it
There's not really anything complicated about PixelOS. Just click around and try out the different apps.
Have fun :)
