# PixelOS

Welcome to PixelOS!

PixelOS is a small operating system that runs in your browser. You can open different apps, move windows around, change the wallpaper, save notes, and mess around with the other features.

## How to use it

When you start PixelOS, you'll see the desktop and some app icons.

Just **click an icon** to open an app. You can also open apps from the taskbar at the bottom.

You can move windows by **clicking and dragging the top bar**.

The red button on a window closes it.

## Apps

### 📝 Notes

Use this to write notes.

* Type something in the box
* Press **Save** to save it
* Press **Clear** to delete it

Your notes are saved in your browser.

### 🧮 Calculator

Pretty much just a normal calculator.

You can use the buttons or your keyboard to enter numbers and basic math.

### 🎨 Theme Lab

This is where you can change how PixelOS looks.

You can:

* Change the accent colour
* Change the wallpaper
* Randomize the theme
* Reset the theme

### 📁 Files

The Files app lets you make a simple list of files.

Enter a file name and press **Create File**. You can also delete files from the list.

These aren't actual files on your computer. They're just saved inside PixelOS.

### 🎵 Music

Pick one of the songs and press **Play**.

You can also pause the music whenever you want.

### ⚙️ Settings

Settings has some extra options for PixelOS.

You can change wallpapers, turn notifications on or off, change sound settings, and open Theme Lab.

## 🌅 Wallpapers

There are a few different wallpapers you can use:

* 🌌 Void
* 🌊 Ocean
* 🌅 Sunset
* 🎨 Other wallpapers in Theme Lab

## 🔔 Notifications

PixelOS has small notifications that pop up when you do certain things, like opening an app or saving a note.

You can turn them off in Settings.

## 💾 Saving

PixelOS uses your browser to save things like notes, files, and theme settings.

So if you close PixelOS and come back later, your saved stuff should still be there.

## That's basically it

There's not really much to learn. Just click around and try stuff.

Have fun with PixelOS :)
